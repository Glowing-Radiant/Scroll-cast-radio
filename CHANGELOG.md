# Changelog

Each version's section is shown in the app's update screen, so write it for listeners.

## 0.4.0
- The update screen is now a full page that lists what's new in every version since yours.
- Music pauses while the update screen is open, and carries on if you choose Later.
- New Audio settings, all off unless you turn them on:
  - Even out loudness keeps every station at a similar volume.
  - Bass boost gives a fuller low end.
  - Equalizer offers Voice clarity for talk and news radio, plus your phone's own presets.

## 0.3.0
- Favorites is now a feed of its own. Swipe, or use headset and lock screen buttons, to move between your favorite stations.
- Going back to the Feed tab carries on where you left off.
- The mood box always opens empty, with your current mood shown underneath.
- Fixed a crash when a narrow feed ran out of stations and started over.

## 0.2.0
- Tell us your mood: type a keyword like hindi, romantic or hip hop and the feed rebuilds around it.
- The app now updates itself from GitHub.
- Region detection, with settings for region, language and genre chosen from lists.
- Separate Feed and Favorites tabs.
